About Us

FORTUNE ASSOCIATION

Fortune Association is a community of the Computer Science and Engineering department where students share their passion for cultural, technical, and sports events. Our mission is to bring students together and create a platform to showcase their talents and skills.

Our Events

Cultural Events

We organize cultural events that celebrate diversity through music, dance, and art performances.

Technical Events

We host technical events where innovators and thinkers showcase ideas and projects.

Sports Events

We organize sports events that promote physical activity, teamwork, and competition.
